# Website Design Brainstorming: Fitroom AI Research Report

## User Request Analysis
The user wants an interactive static webpage to present research findings about Fitroom AI as a supplier for a virtual fitting room business.
Key requirements:
- Modern, professional yet approachable design
- Interactive charts and visualization elements
- Explicitly mention benefits: (a) explore data more intuitively, (b) understand trends better, (c) easily save or share

## Selected Approach
I will proceed with the **Neo-Brutalism meets High Fashion** approach (Response 1), but tempered slightly for professional readability. This style perfectly captures the disruptive nature of AI in the fashion industry. It allows for a "modern, professional yet approachable" design that stands out from typical corporate reports. The "approachable" aspect will be handled through clear data visualization and interactive elements, while the "modern" aspect is inherent in the style.

**Design Philosophy**: "Digital Atelier"
- **Core**: Raw tech meets refined fashion.
- **Colors**: Black/White base, Digital Lavender (#E6E6FA) and Acid Green (#CCFF00) accents.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (Headings) + DM Sans (Body).
- **Visuals**: High-contrast borders, exposed grids, overlapping cards.
