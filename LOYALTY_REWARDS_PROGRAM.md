# StyleSwap Loyalty & Rewards Program
## Complete Guide to Earning and Redeeming Points

---

## Overview

The StyleSwap Loyalty Program rewards customers for engaging with the platform through purchases, referrals, reviews, and social sharing. Points accumulate in user accounts and can be redeemed for credits, exclusive perks, and premium features.

**Program Name:** StyleSwap Rewards  
**Currency:** Points (1 point = R0.01 value)  
**Expiration:** Points never expire  
**Tier System:** Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum

### 2. Referral Rewards (Growth Driver)

**Earning Rate:** 50 points per successful referral

**How It Works:**
1. Customer shares unique referral link (generated automatically)
2. Friend clicks link and creates account
3. Friend makes first credit purchase
4. Original customer receives 50 points
5. Friend receives welcome bonus (varies by season)

**Referral Tiers:**

| Referrals Completed | Bonus Points | Monthly Cap |
|---|---|---|
| 1-5 referrals | 50 points each | 250 points |
| 6-10 referrals | 75 points each | 750 points |
| 11+ referrals | 100 points each | Unlimited |

**Example:** A customer refers 3 friends who sign up and purchase credits. They earn 50 + 50 + 50 = 150 points.

**Referral Bonus Breakdown:**
- Referrer gets: 50 points
- New user gets: 5 free credits (R5 value) OR 50 points (seasonal variation)

**Earning Rate:** 5 points per review

**Review Requirements:**
- Must have completed a try-on of the item
- Review must be at least 50 characters
- Review must include size fit rating (runs small/true/large)
- Review must include overall rating (1-5 stars)

**Review Bonus Tiers:**

| Reviews Per Month | Bonus Multiplier | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 reviews | 1x (standard) | 5 points each |
| 4-7 reviews | 1.5x | 7.5 points each |
| 8+ reviews | 2x | 10 points each |

**Example:** A customer writes 5 reviews in a month. First 3 reviews earn 5 points each (15 total). Remaining 2 reviews earn 7.5 points each (15 total). Monthly total: 30 points.

**Helpful Review Bonus:**
- If review gets 10+ helpful votes: +10 bonus points
- If review gets 25+ helpful votes: +25 bonus points
- Monthly limit: 50 bonus points from helpful reviews

## Fraud Prevention

**Point Earning Restrictions:**
- Cannot earn points from own referral link
- Cannot artificially inflate reviews (flagged by AI)
- Cannot share same content multiple times (tracked)
- Cannot create fake accounts for referrals

**Penalties:**
- Suspicious activity: Points frozen pending review
- Confirmed fraud: Points forfeited, account suspended
- Minor violations: Warning + 50% point deduction

## FAQ: Loyalty & Rewards

**Q: How long do points last?**  
A: Points never expire. They're yours to keep forever.

**Q: Can I lose my tier status?**  
A: No. Once you reach a tier, you keep it for life.

**Q: What's the best way to earn points quickly?**  
A: Combine credit purchases (1 point per R1) with referrals (50 points each) and social sharing (10 points each).

**Q: Can I transfer points to friends?**  
A: No, points are personal and non-transferable.

**Q: What if I redeem all my points?**  
A: You keep your tier status and can start earning again immediately.

**Q: How do I track my points?**  
A: View your points balance and earning history in your dashboard under "Rewards."

**Q: Can I earn points on referred friend's purchases?**  
A: You earn 50 points when they sign up and make their first purchase. You don't earn ongoing points from their activity.

**Q: What's the maximum points I can earn per month?**  
A: There's no hard cap, but most users earn 500-2,000 points monthly depending on activity.

## Summary: The Complete Rewards System

**Earning:** Customers earn points through purchases (1 point per R1), referrals (50 points), reviews (5 points), social sharing (10 points), and boutique purchases (2 points per R100).

**Tiers:** Bronze (0+) → Silver (500+) → Gold (2,000+) → Platinum (5,000+) with increasing redemption bonuses (10%, 20%, 30%) and exclusive perks.

**Redemption:** Points convert to credits at R0.01 per point, with tier bonuses increasing value. Platinum members get 30% extra value on redemptions.

**Lifetime Status:** Once achieved, tier status never downgrades, ensuring long-term customer loyalty and engagement.

**Gamification:** Achievements, seasonal promotions, and milestone bonuses create continuous engagement loops.
