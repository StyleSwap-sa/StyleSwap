# StyleSwap Platform: Boutique Features & Benefits

## Executive Summary

StyleSwap is an AI-powered virtual fitting room platform that transforms how fashion boutiques operate and how customers shop. By integrating cutting-edge generative AI technology with an intuitive boutique management system, StyleSwap enables boutiques to reduce returns by up to 40%, increase conversion rates, and streamline their entire product showcase workflow.

**What It Does:**
- Customers upload their photos and select clothing items from your catalog

- **Builds Customer Confidence:** Eliminates sizing uncertainty and fit concerns

**Customer Experience:**
