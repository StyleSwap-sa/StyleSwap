## 6. Virtual Try-On Service

### 6.1 Service Availability

StyleSwap provides virtual try-on services on an "as-is" basis. While we strive for 99.9% uptime, we do not guarantee uninterrupted service. The Service may be unavailable due to:
- Scheduled maintenance
- Technical issues or bugs
- Third-party service failures (Fitroom AI, AWS, etc.)
- Force majeure events

### 6.2 Image Requirements

To use the virtual try-on feature, you must upload:
- A clear, full-body photograph of the person trying on clothes
- A clear photograph of the clothing item

Images must meet the following requirements:
- Minimum 2048x2048 pixels for body photos
- Minimum 1024x1024 pixels for clothing items
- JPG, PNG, or WebP format
- File size under 10MB
- Clear visibility of the person's body and clothing item

StyleSwap reserves the right to reject images that do not meet these requirements.

### 6.3 Try-On Generation

Try-on generation typically takes 30-60 seconds. In some cases, generation may take up to 3 minutes. If generation fails or times out, credits will be refunded to your account automatically.

### 6.4 Result Quality

While we strive to generate high-quality try-on results, the quality depends on:
- Image quality and clarity
- Lighting and contrast
- Body pose and positioning
- Clothing item characteristics

We do not guarantee perfect results in all cases. Some images may produce better results than others.

### 6.5 Limitations

The virtual try-on feature has the following limitations:
- Works best with standard clothing items (shirts, dresses, jackets)
- May have difficulty with very loose or very tight fitting clothes
- May have difficulty with complex patterns or textures
- Does not account for fabric weight or drape
- Results are approximations, not exact predictions
