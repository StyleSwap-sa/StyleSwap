# StyleSwap Pricing Model

**Effective Date:** January 20, 2026  
**Currency:** South African Rand (ZAR)

---

## Boutique/Merchant Pricing Tiers

StyleSwap offers tiered pricing for boutiques and retail stores based on volume of try-ons purchased.

| Package | Try-Ons | Total Price | Price Per Try-On | Savings |
|---------|---------|-------------|------------------|---------|
| Starter | 100 | R385 | R3.85 | — |
| Growth | 200 | R750 | R3.75 | 2.6% |
| Professional | 500 | R1,350 | R2.70 | 29.9% |
| Business | 1,000 | R2,200 | R2.20 | 42.9% |
| Enterprise | 5,000 | R6,250 | R1.25 | 67.5% |
| Premium | 20,000 | R18,600 | R0.93 | 75.8% |

---

## Individual/B2C Pricing

**Current Model:** Pay-as-you-go  
**Price Per Try-On:** R3.85 (individual customers)

Individual customers can purchase credits at the Starter tier rate or higher if they purchase in bulk.

---

## Credit Validity

- **Validity Period:** 30 days from purchase
- **Auto-Renewal:** Optional (can be configured per boutique)
- **Unused Credits:** Expire after 30 days (no refund)
- **Rollover:** Available upon request for annual contracts

---

## Billing & Payment

**Payment Method:** Yoko Payment Gateway  
**Supported Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express  
**Billing Cycle:** Monthly or as-needed  
**Invoice:** Provided upon request

---

## Volume Discounts & Special Offers

- **Annual Commitment:** 5% additional discount
- **Multi-Year Contract:** 10% additional discount
- **Referral Program:** 10% commission on referred boutiques
- **Non-Profit Organizations:** 20% discount (verification required)

---

## Refund Policy

- **Unused Credits:** Refundable within 7 days of purchase
- **Used Credits:** Non-refundable
- **Disputed Charges:** Investigated within 48 hours
- **Service Issues:** Pro-rata refund if service unavailable >4 hours

---

## Enterprise Pricing

For boutiques requiring:
- Custom integration
- Dedicated support

- API access (future)

## Notes

1. All prices are in South African Rand (ZAR)
2. Prices exclude VAT (if applicable)

5. Credits are non-transferable between boutiques
